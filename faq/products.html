Sophyia products — Sophyia Chat, The Beasts, nOOai — questions & answers

The three products built on the Sophyia orchestration platform — what each one does and who it is for.

What products does Sophyia offer?

Three products on one orchestration platform: Sophyia Chat (a business assistant), The Beasts (an autonomous multi-agent workforce for enterprise) and nOOai (a live multi-agent debate platform). They share the same core — parallel cognition with depth, beyond the single model.

What is Sophyia Chat?

Sophyia Chat is a business assistant deployed with paying customers. It brings Sophyia's multi-agent reasoning to everyday business questions, with answers that are reliable and auditable rather than a single model's guess.

What is The Beasts?

The Beasts is an autonomous multi-agent workforce for the enterprise. Several AI agents take on tasks, work through structured debate and leave full audit trails — designed for work where you need to trace how a conclusion was reached.

What is nOOai?

nOOai is a live multi-agent debate platform: AI instances argue opposing positions in real time, and the result is what survives the debate — useful to explore a question from every angle instead of taking one model's word for it.

Do the three products share the same engine?

Yes. All rest on Sophyia's orchestration core — multiple instances reasoning, contradicting and adjudicating. What changes is the surface: a conversational assistant, an autonomous workforce, or a live debate.

Which product fits my need?

Sophyia Chat for a reliable customer- or team-facing assistant; The Beasts for autonomous, auditable enterprise work; nOOai to explore high-stakes questions through live debate. For guidance on the right fit, get in touch via sophyia.io.
