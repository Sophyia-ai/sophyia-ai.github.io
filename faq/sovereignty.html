Sovereignty & Swiss hosting

Data sovereignty & Swiss hosting — Sophyia — questions & answers

Where Sophyia runs, why Swiss hosting matters, and how auditability serves regulated markets.

Where is Sophyia hosted?

In Switzerland — data and inference run on Swiss soil, on servers in Zurich. Swiss residency is not incidental: it is what makes Sophyia usable by clients for whom data location is non-negotiable.

Why does Swiss data residency matter?

For regulated sectors — finance, legal, public bodies, private banking — where data cannot leave the jurisdiction and every answer may need to be defended. Swiss hosting plus auditability turns AI from a liability into something you can actually deploy.

What does "auditable" mean here?

Every answer comes with its reasoning trail, and when the evidence is thin Sophyia says so and names what is missing rather than producing a confident wrong answer. You can show why the answer is what it is.

Is my data used to train models?

Sophyia's whole positioning rests on sovereignty and trust, with data residency kept in Switzerland. For the exact data-processing terms that apply to your deployment, see the Terms or ask the team directly.

Who is Swiss-hosted, auditable AI for?

Organisations where a confident wrong answer is the real risk and where sovereignty and traceability decide the purchase — fiduciaries, CFOs, legal teams, private banking and public institutions.

How can I learn more?

See the Platform FAQ for how the engine works, and get in touch via sophyia.io for a sovereignty-focused discussion.
